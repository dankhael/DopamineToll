# Dopamine Toll — promo voiceover (33s)

Narration written to sit under `animation.html`. Timecodes are aligned to the
scene windows in `timeline.js`, so each line lands on the motion it describes.
~90 words → a calm ~2.6 words/sec read with pauses. Em dashes and ellipses are
**breath beats**, not spoken.

## Voice direction (for the AI narrator)
- **Character:** warm, low, grounded. Unhurried and a little dry — a friend
  telling you the truth, not an ad. (Gender-neutral, or your brand's choice.)
- **Pace:** measured. Leave a real pause between lines; the visuals breathe.
- **Inflection:** settle each line *down* at the end. No uptalk, no hype lift.
- **Arc:** scenes 1–3 observational; the outro turns a touch warmer and certain.
- **Mix:** sit the music ~ -18 LUFS under the VO; duck it a hair on each line.

## Paste-ready (spoken text only)

Open a site that steals your day — and it stops you before the page even loads.
Your north star. Your own words. And a countdown you can't skip. Then you choose.

Slip past the toll and keep scrolling? It follows you into the feed — your own
reminders, and the minutes you're losing.

You pick the sites. You set the price — how long you wait, how long a pass lasts.

Friction, not a wall — the choice stays yours. It all runs on your device.
Dopamine Toll. Put a price on distraction.

## Notes
- The on-screen captions (kicker + headline per scene) intentionally say
  *different* words from the VO, so the two don't echo each other. The privacy
  line on screen carries "no accounts, no trackers, no network" — the VO only
  says "runs on your device" so it stays short.
- **Localize** like the rest: translate the spoken block per language and record
  a matching `--lang` cut (see `README.md`). Keep the last line's product name
  "Dopamine Toll" in English, matching the store copy.
- If you tighten a scene window in `timeline.js`, re-check that scene's cues here.
